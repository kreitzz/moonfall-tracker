White Whale Proposal · 1H26

Project Yggdrasil

Walleye Order Routing Low-latency Distributed Trans-market Real-time Execution Engine (WorldTree)
In Norse mythology, Yggdrasil is known as the World Tree.

1 The Goal

Goal: Build and launch Yggdrasil — a single unified systematic trading platform that is capable of routing orders across asset classes, markets, and internal systems from DDL. Alongside the engine, ship a well-documented strategy-toolkit library of common helpers and abstractions that lowers the barrier to entry for strategy development, unblocks less technical users, and enables leveraging modern AI tools for strategy authoring.

Background — Current State
Systematic strategies at Walleye are driven by DDL tables of orders that tick, with messages forwarded to our order endpoints. Today this is spread across three distinct services with different communication layers and infrastructure. All share a common challenge: strategy development has a steep learning curve, with no modern, well-documented library of helpers to accelerate development or make strategies accessible to less technical users and AI tooling.
QuantTradingEngine (QTE) EQUITY ~90 strategies enabled daily
Routes OmsOrders, PositionTransfers, and ComboEx Orders through System Equity. Communicates via TCP over the legacy CommLayer.
PRO Activity-key constraints — max orders per key at a given time
PRO Dedicated Strategy QueryConfig type in DDL with its own UI for selecting constants/parameters
PRO DDL latency monitoring that will kill trading strategies if latency is unhealthy
CON Any tick to the order table triggers a cancel/resend cycle — must carefully manage table logic
CON Not redundant to server crashes
VolOrderTransactionEngine (VOTE) EQUITY ~12 strategies enabled daily
Builds on QTE — routes VolTraderOrders with meaningful-difference filtering on updates. Uses Kafka as its messaging layer with status topic output for monitoring.
PRO Meaningful-difference filtering — boolean/enum changes always meaningful; numeric fields use configurable diff thresholds
PRO Squashes noisy updates that come in faster than we can process
CON Specific to VolTrader — missing some of the niceties that QTE exposes
Pyranha + Portainer PANGEA ~2 strategies enabled daily
Supports systematic trading in System Pangea.
CON Requires a separate Portainer service with a listener attached to DDL — adds complexity, additional points of failure, and is difficult to scale

2 Why It Matters

Immediate Impact
Unblocks systematic trading in Pangea: Enables systematic trading for security types and markets not currently supported through System Equity. This could be a huge driver for scaling strategies such as Commodity Vol and FX Vol while also paving the way for automation of key workflows such as FX hedging.
Democratizes strategy development: A well-documented, centralized, unit-tested SDK that many people can contribute to as they come across use cases they think others would find useful.
Consolidation: Reduces the number of bespoke systems into one execution pattern, lowering maintenance burden and operational risk.
Extensibility & Scalability: New execution endpoints and strategy mains become configuration changes. Yggdrasil provides a single platform that grows with the firm rather than a proliferating set of one-off solutions.
Fault tolerance: A more resilient execution platform for systematic strategies — especially critical when trading in off-hours with fewer technologists available to respond to issues.
Lowers barrier to entry on strategy development: Less technical users can build strategies, leverage modern AI tools, and write efficient code without needing deep expertise in the underlying table logic and plumbing.

Future Opportunities
Unlocks FX neutralization via DFDs: Addresses one of the hardest operational challenges in international trading on day one. Additionally, it opens the door for increasing margin efficiency for different currencies at time of trade in the future.
Enables cross-market opportunities: Facilitates strategies like scraping gamma across ADRs and local instruments where we hold derivatives on one or the other. This is an active opportunity in which we are leaving $$ on the table by not doing it, and this project would vastly simplify the means of implementing it.
